Methodology

  • Goal model: Dixon-Coles (1997) bivariate Poisson with a low-score dependence correction (ρ). Each team has an attack and a defence rating; home advantage is a fitted parameter.
  • Recent form: matches are weighted by exp(−ξ × days ago), ξ = 0.0018 per day, so a result loses half its weight after about 13 months.
  • Simulation: every remaining fixture is sampled from the exact score-probability matrix. 200 seasons were simulated under 1 rating vectors drawn from the model’s approximate posterior, so teams with little data carry wider uncertainty.
  • Tie-breaks: points, then goal difference, then goals scored.
  • Data: football-data.co.uk results, seasons synthetic.
